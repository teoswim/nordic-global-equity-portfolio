Macro thesis — the regime this portfolio is built to thrive in

This book is tilted toward a "resilient Nordic, higher-for-longer" macro regime: steady-to-elevated policy rates that reward DNB's net interest margin and the pricing power of quality compounders (Novo Nordisk, Evolution AB), a firm-but-not-booming NOK/oil backdrop that supports Equinor without needing a commodity supercycle, and continued risk-on capital flows into structural growth themes — GLP-1 obesity care, IoT connectivity, iGaming — that Novo Nordisk, Nordic Semiconductor and Evolution are levered to. Investor AB's diversified holding-company structure and the cash buffer are the shock absorbers for the scenarios this book is not built for: a sharp global growth scare (hits Nordic Semiconductor's IoT capex cycle and DNB's credit book together) or a commodity price collapse (hits Equinor and the NOK at once).

Position-limit policy

Hard cap: no single position may exceed 25% of NAV — breaching it turns the row's weight badge red and raises the banner at the top of the page. A 20% weight shows an amber "near limit" warning first. Both thresholds are concentration-risk guardrails, not price targets — a stock crossing the line means the position has grown too large relative to the book, regardless of whether that's good or bad news for the stock itself.
